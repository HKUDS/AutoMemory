- Applied Optoelectronics faces significant financial strain with record cash outflows, highlighting potential risks in the semiconductor and hardware supply chain for tech infrastructure.
- The market narrative for mega-cap tech remains split between strong secular tailwinds in cloud/AI and increasing scrutiny on execution and tangible product rollouts from key players.
