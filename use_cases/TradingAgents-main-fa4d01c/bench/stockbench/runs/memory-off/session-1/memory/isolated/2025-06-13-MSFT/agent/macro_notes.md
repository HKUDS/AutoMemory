## 2025-W24
- Apple's WWDC 2025 failed to excite investors, with its new "Liquid Glass" interface drawing sharp criticism and the stock lacking a positive catalyst, highlighting product cycle risks.
- Apple executives are defending the company's AI strategy and pace, but major Siri features remain delayed a year after announcement, signaling potential execution challenges in a competitive field.
- Oracle projected cloud infrastructure sales to surge over 70% this fiscal year, boosting its shares and indicating continued robust enterprise demand in the cloud sector.
- Amazon's deal with Talen Energy for nuclear power aims to secure carbon-free energy for AWS data centers, addressing both capacity growth and political concerns over electricity prices.
- Amazon continues to be highlighted as a leader in tech due to the strength of its high-growth segments like AWS and digital advertising, despite broader cost-cutting measures.
- Applied Optoelectronics is flagged with a sell rating due to record cash outflows from high capital expenditures, pointing to potential dilution and stress in some tech hardware suppliers.
- The market's focus on AI competition remains intense, with scrutiny on whether Apple's ecosystem integration can keep pace with rivals like Google and OpenAI.
