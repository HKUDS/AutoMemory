## 2025-W23
- Big Tech is driving the S&P 500's recovery, with the Magnificent Seven leading gains and profit engines humming, providing crucial support to the broader equity market.
- The potential return of Trump-era policies, including tariffs and demands for US manufacturing, is emerging as a key macro risk that could disrupt global supply chains and increase costs for major tech firms.
- Central bank policy remains a backdrop for volatility, as some investment funds are positioned defensively with underweights to equities, anticipating ongoing market turbulence.
- A renewed AI investment cycle is evident, with Samsung nearing a deal to integrate Perplexity's AI into its devices, signaling continued cross-industry partnerships and capital deployment into the sector.
- Corporate cost-cutting through automation is accelerating, as Amazon's expanded robotics push aims to unlock billions in savings and reduce labor reliance, a potential tailwind for margins and industrials.
- Analyst sentiment for AI-exposed companies is bullish, with Bank of America raising Amazon's price target citing momentum in AI and robotics, reflecting strong Wall Street confidence in the theme.
- Political rhetoric is influencing market narratives, as businesses may publicly attribute price hikes to proposed tariffs to manage customer sentiment and shield corporate margins.
- Disciplined, risk-managed investment strategies are being emphasized by major funds, with a focus on profit-taking and portfolio adjustments to navigate the current macro environment.
