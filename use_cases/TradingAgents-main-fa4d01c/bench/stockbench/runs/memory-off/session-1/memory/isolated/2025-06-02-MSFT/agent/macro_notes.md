## 2025-W23
- Big Tech, led by Nvidia and Amazon, is driving the S&P 500's recovery as their profit engines and AI-driven efficiency gains show strength.
- The market's resilience is supported by easing trade tensions, solid corporate earnings, and signs of economic cooling, with May marking the S&P 500's best month in a year and a half.
- Lingering macro risks include persistent tariff concerns, particularly for companies like Apple, and Federal Reserve caution which tempers the positive impact of cooling PCE inflation data.
- Central bank policy remains a key focus, as the Fed's cautious stance on rate cuts despite improving inflation data creates uncertainty for equity investors.
- Trade and tax policy are expected to remain Wall Street's primary focus in June, potentially driving sector volatility.
- The semiconductor sector shows long-term momentum, as seen with Marvell's raised price target linked to AI engagements, though limited visibility in custom chip markets presents a risk.
- Consumer staples, exemplified by Coca-Cola, demonstrate resilience and reliable dividends, offering defensive appeal amid economic crosscurrents.
- Amazon represents a key sector tailwind in e-commerce and cloud computing, with strong revenue growth and operational efficiencies underpinning its outlook.
- Stock-specific analysis highlights a rotation narrative, with some investors favoring consistent growth stories like Chipotle over mega-caps like Apple facing tariff headwinds.
- Berkshire Hathaway's concentrated portfolio, heavily invested in dividend stalwarts like Apple and Coca-Cola, signals a continued focus on durable cash-generating businesses.
